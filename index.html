The Veristra approach

Truth lives in the records.

Veristra draws from veritas — truth — and stratum, a layer of record. The name reflects our foundational conviction: reliable findings emerge from examining layered official records, not from inference, surveillance or field observation.

We systematically identify immovable & movable assets, corporate holdings and intellectual property assets across Indian jurisdictions — building a documented foundation for the decisions institutions, counsel and investors cannot afford to get wrong.

Every finding is categorised strictly as confirmed or indicative, in line with the evidentiary standard of the underlying source — so you always know exactly how much weight a finding can bear.

01 / Define

Understand the assignment

We review the objective, target details and the specific questions the final report must help answer. Scope, jurisdictions and turnaround are confirmed in writing.

02 / Map

Build the identity map

Relevant names, identifiers, directorships, partnerships and connected entities are organised into an identity map before any search begins — the foundation of accurate findings.

03 / Search

Examine the record trail

Name and identifier-based checks are conducted across available land, municipal, SRO, regulatory and IP sources — pan-India, jurisdiction by jurisdiction.

04 / Verify

Assess and connect findings

Registered holdings, historical transfers, long-term leases and relevant corporate or IP links are checked for consistency, deduplicated and consolidated into a single view.

05 / Report

Deliver structured intelligence

You receive a clear, evidence-led report with a source appendix — within the agreed 10–14 working-day turnaround.

6. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and while we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain client and engagement information only as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the engagement, maintain required business records, and meet legal or compliance obligations. Information no longer required is securely deleted or archived with restricted access.

Where applicable law permits, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it for a stated purpose. You can make such a request using the contact details below; we may need to verify your identity before acting on it.
